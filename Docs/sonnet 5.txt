TRANCE - SOLO DEV COMMERCIAL RELEASE ESTIMATE
================================================
Date: 2026-08-10
Basis: Design doc ("Trance design document") + current repo POC (Chakra track prototype, Godot)

SCOPE ASSUMPTIONS (confirm before relying on this doc)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Target release: ~1 hour of music-track content = 7 tracks (doc's smallest option)
- Track complexity mix: 3 "Chakra-level" bespoke tracks (first, last, day->night transition)
  + 4 simpler Guitar-Hero-style tracks (single-loop, less scripted, less unique art)
- Includes building a procedural character generator (layered parts, palette variants) for
  the player and festival NPCs, rather than a small fixed hand-drawn cast
- Art and audio (SFX) are OUTSOURCED (freelancers / asset packs); estimate below splits
  YOUR dev hours (integration, tooling, direction, implementation) from the OUTSOURCE
  BUDGET (what you'd pay someone else to produce the assets)
- You already have the music tracks (not estimated here) and are an experienced Godot
  developer (based on the existing POC quality)
- No voice acting assumed (dialogue is text-based, per the doc's UI section)
- No fail state / no lives / no game-over system needed (per design pillar) - this
  meaningfully reduces scope vs a typical rhythm game

Numbers are ranges because solo-dev estimates are inherently uncertain. Treat the
"best guess" as the planning number and the range as your risk buffer.

BIGGEST RISKS TO THIS ESTIMATE
================================================
  - Beatmap authoring is currently done by hand-typing timestamp dictionaries in code
    (see Entities/Rhythm/rhythm.gd) - this does not scale past one song. Building the
    authoring tool (Part 2A) is on the critical path before efficient track production
    can start; skipping it will blow the per-track hours far past the estimate above.
  - The procedural character generator is a classic scope-creep magnet - "good enough"
    variety (a handful of palette-swapped, part-swapped builds) is much cheaper than a
    fully general system. Recommend capping generator ambition early.
  - Outsourcing adds coordination overhead (spec writing, revision rounds) not counted
    in the dollar budget - budget real hours for it, not zero.
  - "No fail state" and text-only dialogue keep scope down; reintroducing either
    (e.g. voice acting, branching dialogue trees, a scoring/lives system) would add
    meaningfully to both the hours and the outsource budget above.
