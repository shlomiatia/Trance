TRANCE — DEVELOPMENT ESTIMATE (v1)
Estimator: Claude Fable 5 | Date: 2026-08-10
Basis: Design doc (Google Drive) + Chakra POC in this repository

ASSUMPTIONS
- Single developer, full time (40 h/week), experienced with Godot (evidenced by POC).
- Target: commercial itch.io release, PC (Windows; Linux/Mac builds are cheap extras in Godot).
- Scope: 7 tracks (~1 hour set) = 3 complex tracks (first, last, day-to-night) + 4 simpler tracks.
- Art style: higher-fidelity pixel art, COMMISSIONED from an artist. Developer hours below cover
  art direction, briefs, review and integration — not producing the art itself. Artist cost is a
  separate external budget (rough ballpark at the end).
- Music exists (user has the track list), but commercial licensing is NOT yet secured — flagged as
  a schedule/scope risk, not estimated as dev hours.
- The POC is a genuine vertical slice (playlist-driven DJ, beat spawning with hit windows, player
  state machine, tutorial, gravity flip, screen rotation, scoring). It proves the concept but is
  hardcoded (beat maps as inline dictionaries, track-name string checks in player logic) and will
  need a data-driven rewrite for production.

3. SCENARIO TOTALS (developer hours)
============================================================

7 TRACKS (primary target: 3 complex + 4 simple, Chakra credited)
  Common:                       1,110–1,650
  Complex: Chakra 120–200 + 2 × (180–300) = 480–800
  Simple:  4 × (58–94)        =   232–376
  TOTAL:                        1,822–2,826 h  =  46–71 weeks  =  ~11–16 months

  BEST GUESS: ~2,200 h ≈ 55 weeks ≈ 12–13 months full time.

14 TRACKS (3–4 complex + 10–11 simple): +~700–900 h  → ~17–21 months
28 TRACKS (3–4 complex + 24–25 simple): +~1,900–2,400 h → ~24–30 months

Estimation confidence notes:
- Biggest risk multipliers: beat-mapping/feel iteration (rhythm games live or die on feel and
  usually need more tuning passes than planned), the character generation system (easy to
  over-scope — consider cutting to hand-made NPC variants), and art iteration latency with an
  external artist (their turnaround gates your integration).
- Solo-dev estimates historically run 1.5–2x optimistic; the high end of the range is the honest
  planning number. Budget 12 months, communicate 15.

4. ART ASSET LIST (for the artist's brief)
============================================================

CHARACTERS (higher-fidelity pixel art, side view; each named character needs a portrait too)
- Player character, male + female variants (or modular): idle, walk, run, jump, fall, land, dash,
  power move, dance x2–3, talk, sit, swim/splash, hang, fly/float, car-seat pose.
  (Largest single character sheet — every rhythm verb needs readable animation.)
- Sahi: idle, walk, dance, talk, shout (Part 7 call-and-response), car driving pose, portrait.
- Kara: idle, walk, dance, talk, portrait.
- Barry: idle, walk, talk, portrait, dance at DOUBLE tempo (extra frames or 2x playback design).
- Unnamed: idle, walk, talk (rare), portrait, 3–4 distinct dance animations (she communicates
  through dance — her animation set is her dialogue).
- Blackness (amorphous, tracks only): amorphous idle/flow, engulf/appear/disappear, 2–3
  track-specific behaviors. Likely shader + sprite hybrid.
- Performers: drummer (kit + play-on-beat cycle), guitarist, singer — each with on-beat
  accent frames so they visibly sync to the music.
- NPC festival-goers: modular parts (bodies × heads × hair × clothing) OR ~10–15 hand-made
  variants, each with idle, walk, 2–3 dance loops. (Recommend hand-made variants over a
  generator — much cheaper and the doc only needs "many NPCs, most not interactable".)
- Minor one-off characters: ~3–5 with idle/talk/portrait.

ENVIRONMENT — FESTIVAL HUB
- Ground/terrain tileset: grass, dirt path, sand/desert, lakeshore.
- Location set pieces: festival entrance/gate, hill, small lake (jumpable), tent cluster, desert
  backdrop, big lake + boat, 2+ stages (speaker stacks, lighting rigs, DJ booth), food court
  stalls, misc: flags, tapestries, shade sails, campfires, trash cans, art installations,
  portable toilets, parked cars.
- Parallax background layers: distant mountains/desert horizon, sky.
- TIME-OF-DAY: all of the above in 4 lighting states (day/dusk/night/dawn) — via palette swap or
  lighting/shader if the art is built for it (agree on the approach with the artist EARLY; it
  roughly multiplies environment cost otherwise).
- Sky sets: day, dusk, night (stars), dawn; moon/sun; stage lights and laser effects at night.

ENVIRONMENT — TRACK LEVELS (bespoke, per complex track)
- Chakra: exaggerated entrance/hill/lake versions, cloudscape world, giant bird (flying,
  swooping), free-fall sky backgrounds, rotation-safe environment pieces.
- Other 2 complex tracks: comparable bespoke sets (space/jungle/etc. per final design) —
  budget ~1 environment set + 1–2 unique creatures/characters each.
- Simple tracks: 1 backdrop variation + palette shift each, reusing performers/VFX.

UI
- Beat indicators: neutral, left, right, center (+ up/down if added), hit state, miss state,
  approach/highlight states.
- Target line / hit zone marker; power-move prompt.
- Dialogue box (9-slice), speaker name/color tags, portrait frame, talk-availability indicator,
  conversation-continue arrow.
- Results screen layout: track title/artist/length, score, grade (S/A/B/C…), retry prompt.
- Main/pause menu backgrounds, buttons, sliders, focus states; controller + KB/M button glyphs.
- Title/logo art, credits layout, font(s) licensed for commercial use.

VFX (artist and/or shader work by dev)
- On-beat hit particles/burst, miss marker, combo/streak accents.
- Player trail (POC has one), dash effect, landing dust, water splash.
- Psychedelic track effects: background pulse-to-BPM, kaleidoscope/color-cycling shaders,
  screen-rotation transition, grey-out effect for miss streaks, day-night transition effect.
- Crowd ambience animation (bobbing silhouettes), stage lights/lasers/smoke.

MARKETING / STORE
- itch.io cover image (630×500), banner, 6–10 screenshots, animated GIFs, trailer (30–60 s),
  game icon, press-kit sheet.

ROUGH ARTIST BUDGET (external cost, not dev hours):
The list above is plausibly 500–900 artist-hours for good higher-fidelity pixel art.
At typical commissioned pixel-art rates ($20–50/h or per-asset equivalent): ~$12k–$40k.
Cutting the NPC generator, buying a font, and limiting time-of-day to palette swaps pulls this
toward the low end. This is the biggest cash cost of the project — get quotes early.

============================================================
5. AUDIO ASSET LIST (music excluded — you have the list)
============================================================

Design constraint from the doc: track levels use ONLY the music; hit/miss feedback is visual.
So the SFX list is small and hub-focused:

- Footsteps: grass, sand, dirt (2–4 variations each); run variants.
- Jump, land (soft/hard), dash whoosh.
- Water: small splash (lake jump), big splash, swimming loop.
- Conversation blips: per-character voice-tone blips (Sahi, Kara, Barry, player; Unnamed gets
  none or a hummed tone) — cheap way to add personality.
- UI: menu move/select/back, pause in/out, results screen sting (optional — doc says minimal).
- Festival ambience: crowd walla loop (day + night variants), wind/desert, water lapping,
  night crickets, birds (dawn), campfire crackle.
- Car: engine idle/driving loop for the ending scene.
- Music-system audio engineering (dev/producer time, already partly counted in per-song rows):
  slicing each track into loopable parts, loudness normalization across tracks, low-volume
  "festival PA" mixdowns of tracks for hub background, muffled/filtered versions for
  distance-from-stage effect.

MUSIC LICENSING (flag, not estimated): commercial release requires written sync licenses from
every artist/label. Psytrance labels often control masters. This can take weeks-to-months of
calendar time and may cost per track — start now, in parallel with development, and have backup
tracks in case a license falls through. Do not build 3 complex bespoke tracks around music you
don't have rights to yet.

============================================================
6. SUGGESTED BUILD ORDER
============================================================
1. Rhythm engine rewrite + beat tooling (everything depends on it).
2. Finish Chakra on the new engine with placeholder art (proves the pipeline end-to-end).
3. Festival hub + conversation + time-of-day with 2–3 tracks.
4. Commission art once systems are stable (avoid re-briefing).
5. Remaining tracks in parallel with art integration.
6. Menus/save/options/release engineering last 2 months + polish.
